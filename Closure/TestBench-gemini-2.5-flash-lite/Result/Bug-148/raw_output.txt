## SOURCE CODE ANALYSIS
The tests cover `tryFoldTypeof`, `tryFoldUnaryOperator` (for `NOT`, `NEG`, `BITNOT`), `tryFoldInstanceof`, `tryFoldAssign` (for shorthand assignments), `tryFoldAndOr`, `tryFoldLeftChildAdd`, `tryFoldAddConstant`, `tryFoldArithmetic`, `tryFoldBitAndOr`, `tryFoldShift`, `tryFoldComparison`, `tryFoldGetElem`, `tryFoldGetProp`, `tryFoldStringJoin`, `tryFoldStringIndexOf`.

## TEST CASE DESIGN
- `testTypeOf`: Checks behavior of `typeof` operator with various literal types.
- `testTypeOfName`: Checks `typeof` with the name "undefined".
- `testNot`: Folds logical NOT operations on boolean and numeric literals.
- `testNeg`: Folds negation of numeric literals.
- `testBitNot`: Folds bitwise NOT on integer literals within the 32-bit range.
- `testBitNotOutOfBounds`: Tests that `~` on out-of-bounds numbers is not folded and reports an error.
- `testBitNotFractional`: Tests that `~` on fractional numbers is not folded and reports an error.
- `testInstanceof`: Folds `instanceof Object` for immutable values.
- `testAssignAdd`: Folds compound assignment operators (e.g., `x = x + y` to `x += y`).
- `testAndOr`: Folds short-circuiting `&&` and `||` operators with literal operands.
- `testLeftChildAdd`: Folds `ADD` operations where the left child is also an `ADD` and its right child is a string.
- `testAddConstant`: Folds `ADD` operations with constant operands (numeric or string concatenation).
- `testArithmetic`: Folds basic arithmetic operations (`+`, `-`, `*`, `/`) with numeric literals.
- `testArithmeticOutOfBounds`: Ensures arithmetic folding doesn't occur for numbers exceeding `MAX_FOLD_NUMBER` due to potential precision loss.
- `testDivideByZero`: Tests that division by zero is detected and reports an error.
