- `testParseCompactDateOnly`: compact date; same epoch and advances 8 characters.
- `testParseUtcTime`: full UTC timestamp at epoch; advances 20 characters.
- `testParseTimeWithoutSeconds`: omitted seconds default to zero; timestamp is epoch.
- `testParseCompactTime`: compact time fields are zero; timestamp is epoch.
- `testParseFractionOneDigit`: fraction `.1` is scaled to 100 ms.
- `testParseFractionTwoDigits`: fraction `.12` is scaled to 120 ms.
- `testParseFractionThreeDigits`: fraction `.123` is 123 ms.
- `testParsePositiveTimezone`: +01:00 means 01:00 local corresponds to epoch UTC.
- `testParseNegativeTimezone`: -01:00 means 23:00 local corresponds to epoch UTC.
- `testParseTimezoneWithoutColon`: +0100 yields the same UTC instant as +01:00.
- `testParseTimezoneHourOnly`: +01 is extended to +0100 and yields epoch.
- `testParseLeapSecondIsTruncated`: second 60 is clamped to 59, yielding 59000 ms.
- `testParseTrailingTextAdvancesPositionThroughZone`: parser consumes the valid timestamp and zone, leaving trailing text unconsumed.
- `testParseStartingAtNonzeroPosition`: parsing begins at index 2 and ends at index 12.
- `testParseInvalidDayThrowsParseException`: strict calendar rejects February 30; wrapped parse exception retains the initial error offset.
- `testParseInvalidTimezoneThrowsParseException`: invalid timezone indicator is wrapped as `ParseException` at the initial offset.
