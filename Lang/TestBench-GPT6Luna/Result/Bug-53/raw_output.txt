    public void testSameDayIgnoresTime() throws Exception {

        a.set(2020, Calendar.JANUARY, 2, 1, 0, 0);
        Calendar b = (Calendar) a.clone();

        a.clear();
        a.set(2020, Calendar.JANUARY, 2);

- `testAddWeeks`: one week added; matches the corresponding calendar-field addition.
- `testAddDays`: one day after epoch is 86,400,000 ms.
- `testAddHours`: one hour after epoch is 3,600,000 ms.

- `testAddSeconds`: one second after epoch is 1,000 ms.
- `testAddMilliseconds`: one millisecond after epoch is 1 ms.

- `testRoundDateAtMinuteHalf`: 03:30 rounds up to 04:00; expected time adds one hour.
