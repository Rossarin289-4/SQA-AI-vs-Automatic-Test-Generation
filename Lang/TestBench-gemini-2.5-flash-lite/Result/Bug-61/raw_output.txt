Testing inner classes like `StrBuilderTokenizer`, `StrBuilderReader`, and `StrBuilderWriter` would require more setup or direct instantiation not explicitly allowed by the prompt.
Actual defect detection is unknown until the unchanged test suite is executed against the reference and the defective versions.
