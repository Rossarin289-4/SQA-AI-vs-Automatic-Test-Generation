### Identify

1. **The behavior that differs:**
   In `FastDateParser.escapeRegex`, whitespace characters in constant or literal parts of date patterns (such as spaces between tokens or literal text containing spaces) are handled differently. In the buggy version, whenever a sequence of whitespace characters is encountered, `escapeRegex` appends `"\\s*+"` (possessive zero-or-more whitespace matcher) to the regular expression. In the fixed version, the whitespace handling block (`if (Character.isWhitespace(c)) { ... }`) was completely removed from `escapeRegex`, meaning literal whitespace characters in the pattern are treated as standard characters or matched as authored without automatically collapsing or turning arbitrary whitespace sequences into `\\s*+`.

2. **The likely root cause visible from the source:**
   The buggy `escapeRegex` method contains explicit special-casing for whitespace (`Character.isWhitespace(c)`), which tracks `wasWhite` and replaces whitespace chunks with `\\s*+`. This causes `FastDateParser` to improperly parse dates where exact whitespace formatting or non-collapsing literal separators are expected, or where `\\s*+` alters expected matching semantics of literal characters and whitespace in format patterns.

3. **The specific behavior that a generated test should distinguish between the buggy and fixed versions:**
   When parsing date strings with specific literal separators containing whitespace (or multiple spaces / tabs vs single spaces), the buggy version's insertion of `\\s*+` causes it to accept arbitrary amounts of whitespace or behave inconsistently with `SimpleDateFormat` and literal expectations, whereas the fixed version correctly respects the literal pattern structure or handles whitespace and regex escaping precisely without injecting unauthorized possessive whitespace quantifiers.

4. **Boundary cases, representative cases, and regression cases that are independently designed:**
   - Parsing a date pattern with explicit multiple spaces or specific literal text containing whitespace separating date fields (e.g., `"yyyy-MM-dd HH:mm:ss"` vs patterns with tabs or literal words separated by spaces).
   - Verifying that parsing strict literal spacing behaves correctly and does not incorrectly swallow or expand whitespace due to `\\s*+`.

- **Test 1: `testParserWithLiteralWhitespaceSeparator`**
  - **Pattern / Input:** Uses a pattern containing literal words and spaces (e.g., `"yyyy 'at' HH:mm"`) with a source string containing differing whitespace or exact matching constraints.
  - **Buggy Version:** Injects `\\s*+`, causing unexpected matching behavior or lenient whitespace handling where strict/exact literal matching is expected.
  - **Fixed Version:** Correctly parses according to standard strict regex escaping rules without arbitrary `\\s*+` insertion.

- **Test 2: `testParserWithMultipleSpacesInPattern`**
  - **Pattern / Input:** Uses a format pattern containing multiple consecutive spaces between fields (e.g., `"yyyy    MM    dd"`).
  - **Buggy Version:** Collapses multiple spaces into `\\s*+` which matches any whitespace of arbitrary length.
  - **Fixed Version:** Treats literal spaces properly according to standard pattern matching behavior.
