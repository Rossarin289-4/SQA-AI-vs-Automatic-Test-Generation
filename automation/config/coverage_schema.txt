COVERAGE CSV SCHEMA
===================

File:
coverage.csv

Format:

metric,value

Required metrics:

line_coverage
branch_coverage

Example:

metric,value
line_coverage,75.0
branch_coverage,61.5

Coverage must be collected separately for:

BUGGY
FIXED

The same Defects4J coverage mechanism must be used for:
- ChatGPT
- Gemini
- SA
- BPSO

Coverage is descriptive evidence.
It does not determine whether a test detects the bug.

Defects4J coverage reports:
- LinesTotal
- LinesCovered
- ConditionsTotal
- ConditionsCovered

Derived:
line_coverage =
LinesCovered / LinesTotal * 100

branch_coverage =
ConditionsCovered / ConditionsTotal * 100
