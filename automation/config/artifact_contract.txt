EXPERIMENT ARTIFACT CONTRACT
============================

For each method and bug:

Lang/<METHOD>/Result/Bug-<BUG>/

Required canonical artifacts:

experiment.json
prompt.txt
raw_output.txt
generated_test.java
evaluation.json
evaluation.csv
checksums.sha256

Buggy/
    execution.txt
    coverage.csv

Fixed/
    execution.txt
    coverage.csv

Optional / method-specific evidence may also exist, for example:
    config.txt
    generation_info.txt
    selected_inputs.txt
    test_results.csv
    execution_raw.txt
    execution_summary.csv
    coverage_execution.txt
    compile.txt
    test_compile.txt
    fdr_summary.csv
    raw_source.sha256
    evaluated_source.sha256
    source_snapshot/
    archive/
    test_suite/

DONE requires all canonical artifacts above to exist and contain valid data.

Traceability:
results/master_results.csv
    |
    +--> Lang/<METHOD>/Result/Bug-<BUG>/experiment.json
             |
             +--> generated_test.java
             +--> prompt.txt
             +--> raw_output.txt
             +--> evaluation.json
             +--> evaluation.csv
             +--> Buggy/execution.txt
             +--> Buggy/coverage.csv
             +--> Fixed/execution.txt
             +--> Fixed/coverage.csv
             |
             +--> projects/Lang/Bug-<BUG>/diff.txt

Canonical coverage schema is defined in automation/config/coverage_schema.txt.
Method-specific artifacts must not replace or alter the canonical artifacts.
