- `testDatesRoundDownToWholeSeconds`: milliseconds `1234` map to `1000`; `-1` milliseconds use Java integer division and map to `0`.
- `testDateSetterAcceptsSignedIntegerMaximum`: maximum signed second value; conversion preserves `Integer.MAX_VALUE`.
