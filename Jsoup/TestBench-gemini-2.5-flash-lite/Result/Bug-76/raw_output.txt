    - testBeforeHtmlIgnoresDoctype: Input: Doctype token. Expected: false (error). Derived: Logic in BeforeHtml.process.

    - testBeforeHtmlHandlesAnythingElseByInsertingHtml: Input: StartTag("p"). Expected: true, html inserted, state transitions to BeforeHead. Derived: Logic in BeforeHtml.anythingElse.
    - testBeforeHeadIgnoresWhitespace: Input: Whitespace token. Expected: true (ignored). Derived: Logic in BeforeHead.process.
