**LIMITATIONS**
Some tests rely on the default behavior of `ObjectMapper` or its components (like `ServiceLoader` for `findAndRegisterModules`) or require specific external setup (like a test resource file for `readTreeURL`). Testing internal logic of `DeserializationProblemHandler` or `TypeModifier` is difficult without mocking.
Actual defect detection is unknown until the unchanged test suite is executed against the reference and the defective versions.
