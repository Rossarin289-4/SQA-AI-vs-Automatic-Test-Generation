Boundary values for integer types (min/max) are tested. The `append` method is tested with two separate buffers. `toString` is tested for both empty and populated buffers. `firstToken` is tested for empty and non-empty buffers. `close` and `isClosed` are tested, ensuring the buffer remains parsable. Unsupported operations like `writeRaw...` and `writeBinary` are tested to ensure they throw `UnsupportedOperationException`.
4. DEFECT DETECTION STRATEGY - The tests cover the core functionality of writing and reading tokens, verifying that the `TokenBuffer` correctly stores and retrieves data. Testing unsupported operations ensures that the expected exceptions are thrown.
