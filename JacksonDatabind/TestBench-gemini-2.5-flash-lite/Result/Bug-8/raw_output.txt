**1. SOURCE CODE ANALYSIS**
The tests focus on the `CreatorCollector` class, specifically its methods for collecting and configuring different types of creators (default, string, int, long, double, boolean, delegating, and property-based). The `constructValueInstantiator` method and various `add*Creator` methods, along with `verifyNonDup` and `_fixAccess` helper methods, are tested. The `Vanilla` inner class is also tested for its default instantiation behavior.
