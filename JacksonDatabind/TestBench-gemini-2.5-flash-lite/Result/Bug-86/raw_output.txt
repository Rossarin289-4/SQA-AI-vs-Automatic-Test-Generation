The tests target `setReference`, `getSelfReferencedType`, `getSuperClass`, `getGenericSignature`, `getErasedSignature`, `toString`, and `equals`. They also cover the methods that should return `this` instance (`withContentType`, `withTypeHandler`, etc.) and edge cases like unresolved types and multiple `setReference` calls.
