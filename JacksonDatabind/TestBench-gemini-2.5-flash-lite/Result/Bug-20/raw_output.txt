LIMITATIONS - The tests assume the availability and correct instantiation of `JsonNodeFactory` via `JsonNodeFactory.instance`, which is not explicitly provided in the API outline and has a protected constructor. Actual defect detection is unknown until the unchanged test suite is executed against the reference and the defective versions.
